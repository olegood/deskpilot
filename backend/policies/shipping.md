# Shipping and delivery

## Delivery times

Standard delivery is three to five working days within the destination region.
Express delivery is one to two working days.

Working days exclude weekends and public holidays in the destination country.
Orders placed after 14:00 local time ship the next working day.

## Tracking

Every shipped order has a tracking number, which is available as soon as the
carrier scans the parcel. A tracking number that shows no scans yet usually means
the parcel is packed but has not been collected.

## Delayed parcels

A parcel is considered delayed once it is five working days past its expected
delivery date with no new carrier scan.

A parcel with no carrier scan for 10 consecutive days is treated as lost in
transit, and the customer is offered a refund or a replacement without needing to
return anything.

## Parcels marked delivered but not received

When the carrier reports a parcel as delivered and the customer has not received
it, ask the customer to check with neighbours and any safe place, and to wait 24
hours, because carriers sometimes scan a parcel as delivered before the final drop.

If it is still missing after 24 hours, open a carrier investigation. Acme Gear does
not refund a parcel marked delivered until the carrier investigation is complete,
which takes up to 10 working days.

## Wrong or incomplete deliveries

If the wrong item arrived, or an item is missing from the order, Acme Gear sends
the correct item immediately and pays for the return of anything sent in error.
