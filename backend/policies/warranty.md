# Warranty

## What is covered

Acme Gear equipment carries a two-year warranty against manufacturing defects,
counted from the delivery date. That covers failed seams, delaminating fabric,
broken buckles and zips, and electronics that stop working in normal use.

## What is not covered

Wear and tear from normal use is not a defect: worn-through soles, faded fabric,
scuffed finishes.

Damage from an accident, misuse, or an unsuitable repair is not covered. Neither is
damage from storing equipment wet.

## How a warranty claim is handled

The customer sends photographs of the fault. If the defect is clear from the
photographs, a replacement is sent without requiring the item back.

If it is not clear, the item is returned for inspection at our cost, and the
outcome is a repair, a replacement, or a refund.

## Warranty outcomes

Within 6 months of delivery, a confirmed defect is refunded or replaced at the
customer's choice.

After 6 months, a repair or a replacement is offered first. A refund is offered
only when neither is possible, and it may be reduced to reflect the use the
customer has had.
