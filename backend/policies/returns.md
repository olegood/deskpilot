# Returns

## Standard return window

Customers may return most items within 30 days of delivery for any reason,
including simply changing their mind. The 30 days are counted from the delivery
date recorded by the carrier, not from the order date.

Gold tier customers have 60 days instead of 30.

## Condition of returned items

Returned items must be unused and in their original packaging, with any tags still
attached. Items that show signs of use on the trail - mud, scuffs, odour, pet hair -
are treated as used and are handled under the warranty policy instead.

Safety-critical equipment that has been used cannot be returned, because we cannot
resell it: climbing hardware, avalanche transceivers, and helmets.

## Items that cannot be returned

Hygiene items cannot be returned once opened. That covers hydration bladders,
sleeping bag liners, and base layers.

Gift cards and digital guidebooks are not returnable.

## Who pays for return shipping

If the item is faulty, damaged on arrival, or we sent the wrong thing, Acme Gear
pays for return shipping and sends a prepaid label.

If the customer is returning for any other reason, the customer pays return
shipping. Gold tier customers get free return shipping on every return.
