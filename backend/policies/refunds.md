# Refunds

## When a refund is issued

A refund is issued once a returned item arrives at the warehouse and passes
inspection, which normally takes two working days after delivery.

For a parcel confirmed lost in transit, a refund is issued immediately, with no
return required.

For a faulty item, the customer chooses between a refund and a replacement.

## How much is refunded

A refund covers the price paid for the item, at the price on the order rather than
the current catalogue price.

Original outbound shipping is refunded only when the whole order is returned, or
when the return is our fault: a faulty item, damage on arrival, or the wrong item
sent.

Return shipping is refunded only in the cases listed in the returns policy.

## Partial refunds

If only some items in an order are returned, only those items are refunded.

A partial refund of up to 20% of the item price may be offered as a goodwill
gesture when a customer keeps an item they are unhappy with, instead of returning
it. Anything above 20% needs a supervisor.

## Refund method and timing

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Acme Gear cannot refund to a
different card, a bank transfer, or store credit at the customer's request.

Once issued, a refund takes three to five working days to appear on the customer's
statement. That delay is with the customer's bank and cannot be shortened.

## Refund limits

A single refund above 500.00 in the order's currency needs supervisor approval.

More than three refunds for the same customer in 90 days needs supervisor approval,
whatever the amounts.
